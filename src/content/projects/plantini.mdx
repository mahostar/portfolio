## Problem
A hydroponic product connects water quality, sensing, dosing, physical structures, and software. Plantini brings those parts together in a system designed and fabricated as one product.

## What I built
As a co-founder, I worked on proprietary AC-based TDS sensing, ESP32 firmware, and the CAD design of agricultural farms and hydroponic towers. Towers were manufactured in-house using 3D printing. The project also includes nutrient-water treatment research.

## How it works
Sensor readings feed the embedded controller. Firmware maps the sensor and actuator wiring, while the tower geometry and fabrication process support the physical growing system. The sensing design remains proprietary; this page describes the system at a high level.

## Result
Plantini brings together a physical hydroponic tower, custom sensing, farm CAD, and in-house fabrication. The sensing and firmware work includes calibration around a 1,000 ppm dosing target.

## What I would improve
A useful next step is a documented long-duration calibration and dosing study, including sensor drift, maintenance, and variation between assembled units.

## Problem
Selling printed products brings the storefront, custom-order process and physical fabrication together.

## What I built
Faza3D is an online shop for products manufactured with a fleet of 3D printers. The supplied record shows the storefront, catalogue, custom-order form and printing equipment.

## How it works
Customers can browse the catalogue or request a custom print. The manufacturing record includes Bambu Lab, Creality and Ultimaker equipment described in the project notes.

## Result
The gallery documents the shop interface, printer setup and a finished articulated print.

The next update will add the live store link, project timeline and a detailed account of the ordering and fulfilment workflow.

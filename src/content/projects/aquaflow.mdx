## Abstract
AquaFlow connects a fish-water prototype, in-house conductivity-based sensing, an Arduino Nano measurement processor, an ESP32 network/controller layer, and a mobile monitoring interface. The supplied firmware implements a framed single-wire pulse handoff for TDS and temperature, local ultrasonic distance sensing, a freshness flag, and separate cloud ownership for measurements and requested relay states. The physical explanation relates feeding, recirculation, filtration, and water-quality interpretation. This paper distinguishes bulk ionic monitoring from ammonia-specific measurement and separates mineral adsorption from biological nitrification. The diagrams describe the prototype and inspected firmware without publishing proprietary sensing parameters or claiming unmeasured water-treatment performance.

**Keywords:** aquaculture monitoring; conductivity proxy; single-wire encoding; dual-core controller; state ownership; recirculation; filtration.

## Method
### 3.1 Feeding changes both solid and dissolved loads
Feed supports fish growth, while uneaten material and metabolic waste add loads to the water. Suspended material can be captured mechanically. Dissolved nitrogen requires a different interpretation: ammonium-binding mineral media and biological conversion have distinct mechanisms and operating dependencies.

Mineral adsorption depends on the material and water chemistry. Zeolite and bentonite have been studied for nitrogen-compound adsorption under aquaculture-relevant conditions; that background does not identify AquaFlow's exact filter material or establish its removal capacity. [Şahin et al., mineral adsorption study](https://www.academicoa.com/ILNS.71.34).

Biological filtration transforms ammonia through nitrite to nitrate rather than simply trapping it as a solid:

$$
\mathrm{NH_3/NH_4^+}\longrightarrow
\mathrm{NO_2^-}\longrightarrow\mathrm{NO_3^-}.
\tag{1}
$$

Biofilter operation and fish welfare also depend on oxygen and other water conditions. Temperature, pH, dissolved oxygen, nitrogen compounds, and species-specific needs therefore cannot be replaced by one TDS threshold. [FAO water-reuse and biological treatment background](https://www.fao.org/4/y5042e/y5042e07.htm).

### 3.2 What the TDS path measures
AquaFlow reuses the in-house sensing foundation developed for Plantini. Its alternating measurement approach yields a conductivity-related response, interpreted through temperature context and calibration. In general,

$$
\begin{aligned}
\kappa_{25}&\approx\frac{\kappa_T}{1+\alpha(T-25)},\\
C_{\mathrm{TDS}}&\approx f_{\mathrm{solution}}\kappa_{25}.
\end{aligned}
\tag{2}
$$

The conversion factor depends on ionic composition. TDS is useful as a bulk ionic trend and calibrated operating indicator; it is not a direct ammonia, dissolved-oxygen, or individual-contaminant measurement. Filtering solids, adsorbing ammonium, and changing dissolved salt composition may affect different measurements in different ways. [USGS specific-conductance background](https://water.usgs.gov/nwc/NWC/water_quality/tables/quality.abs.html).

### 3.3 Framed pulse communication
The supplied `nano.ino` and `esp.ino` pair use a one-wire pulse-width message, rather than UART or I²C. The semantic order is a start marker, TDS field, separator, temperature field, and end marker. The receiver checks timing boundaries and waits for the required transitions before decoding the two values.

The public figure preserves field order and receiver responsibilities, but omits pin assignments, pulse constants, sensing circuitry and calibration coefficients. The transport should not be described as checksum-protected or acknowledged: those guarantees are not implemented in the inspected path.

## Implementation
### 4.1 Local sensing and freshness
The ESP32's main loop receives Nano packets and measures ultrasonic distance. Accepted packets update the retained TDS and temperature values and record the last packet time. Let $n_r$ count accepted packets and $\Delta t=t-t_{\mathrm{last}}$ denote their age. The Nano-link flag $a_N$ becomes false after more than five seconds without an accepted packet:

$$
a_N(t)=\mathbf{1}[n_r>0]\,\mathbf{1}[\Delta t\le5\ \mathrm s].
\tag{3}
$$

The stored numeric readings remain available after link loss. The freshness flag must accompany them; a retained value alone should not be presented as a newly measured state. Invalid ultrasonic readings are published as unavailable rather than as a physical distance.

### 4.2 Two execution contexts
The networking task is pinned to core 0, while the local sensor loop runs on core 1. A critical section protects snapshots of the shared measurement values. This isolates responsibilities and avoids holding that lock through an HTTP request; it does not establish hard real-time behavior or eliminate every timing interaction.

The cloud task targets sensor publication and relay polling at approximately one-second intervals. HTTP duration, reconnects, task scheduling and local acquisition add delay. This interval is a configured cadence, not a measured end-to-end latency guarantee.

### 4.3 Separate measurement and command ownership
The author describes the applications as Firebase-connected. The supplied ESP32 revision implements that cloud boundary through Firestore REST: it PATCHes a sensor document and GETs a separate relay document. Its data flow is therefore specified from the actual firmware, rather than inferred as a Realtime Database stream or MQTT subscriber.

The separation addresses an explicit source comment about overwritten app changes. The device publishes measurements and listens for requested pump, compressor and UV states; it does not continually republish its old relay choices over the user's desired state. Changed cloud choices trigger local relay application.

Boot initializes relay outputs off, then fetches stored choices if connected. On a later cloud failure, the inspected polling function returns without applying a new state. It does not provide a documented per-command acknowledgement, automatic stale-command shutdown, or verified actuator feedback. Desired state and observed hardware state must therefore remain distinct concepts.

They do not invent treatment measurements, fish-growth outcomes, or an implemented AI feeding policy from interface labels.

## Discussion
The most substantial software decision is ownership: measurement state flows outward from the device, while desired actuator choices flow inward from the shared cloud state. Separating those paths avoids a common feedback race in which a device overwrites a fresh operator command with its previous local state.

The next evaluation layer would measure packet acceptance, link-loss behavior, cloud-to-output delay and actual output feedback over a recorded run. On the water side, nutrient/contaminant-specific testing, filter loading, oxygen availability and feeding load matter alongside bulk TDS trends. The prototype makes sensing and equipment operation visible; its performance should be assessed against those distinct physical and communication requirements.

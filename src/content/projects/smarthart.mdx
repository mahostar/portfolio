## Problem
A monitoring system needs to detect changes across several vital signs and display them clearly over time. SmartHart explores that pipeline with generated physiological data.

## What I built
I built a data generator, a BiLSTM anomaly-detection model, and a Tkinter dashboard for blood pressure, heart rate, oxygen saturation, and respiration. The repository contains separate generation, training, and model-running tools.

## How it works
The generator creates timestamped vital-sign sequences with anomaly scenarios. A two-layer BiLSTM learns from those sequences. The dashboard runs the model on incoming windows and visualizes the readings and predictions.

## Result
The project reports 98.34% accuracy and AUC 1.00 in its generated-data evaluation, with about 93% accuracy on a ten-point window. These results concern a research prototype and synthetic data; they are not clinical validation. Reinforcement-learning resource optimization remains future work.

## What I would improve
The next study should test external, properly sourced datasets, quantify false alarms, and evaluate missing or noisy readings. Clinical use would require a separate validation process.

## Problem
Generating a 3D-printable mathematical surface requires control over its geometry, wall thickness and output mesh.

## What I built
I developed a Python engine for Triply Periodic Minimal Surface structures, with controllable cell repetition, level-set parameters and wall thickness.

## How it works
The engine combines implicit surface generation, mesh extraction, optimization, validation and binary STL export.

## Result
The supplied screenshots show several generated mesh structures. The workflow is designed to keep the exported models within practical file-size limits.

## What I would improve
A future update will include exported examples, print results and documented validation checks for representative geometries.
